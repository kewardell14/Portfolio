About

Project Overview

This project focused on developing a screen-accurate Sabacc deck inspired by The Mandalorian & Grogu, combining recognizable in-universe details with an original visual concept. The challenge was to create a high-quality, playable game that appealed to existing Sabacc fans while introducing new players to the game through a familiar Star Wars setting. The packaging became an extension of the game's story, exploring how the New Republic repurposed Imperial materials and transforming that history into an interactive element of the unboxing experience.

Project Scope

  • Designed a complete Sabacc deck, including three numbered suits, specialty cards, a Commander Coin, a card back, and a number legend.
  • Developed an original visual direction for the cards and packaging, including custom Aurebesh numerals, color, layout, and Imperial imagery.
  • Designed the interior and exterior packaging, incorporating a scratch-reveal concept that connects the New Republic's present to the deck's Imperial origins.
